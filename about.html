About

I build the systems that turn signups into revenue.

Most lifecycle marketing looks like email. What I actually build is closer to a behavioral decision engine: who gets what message, when, based on what they've done — and what that combination is most likely to make them do next.

I've spent my career in the post-signup part of the funnel, where the product and the marketing motion have to work together. That's made me comfortable operating at the intersection of data, product, and copy in ways that pure-channel marketers usually aren't. I can pull the SQL, design the experiment, write the email, and explain the result to a product manager and a CEO in the same meeting.

My background spans B2B2C marketplaces (Snagajob), regulated financial products (Wells Fargo, Conduent), and product-led SaaS (TurboTenant). The common thread across all three: a paying business benefits when end users take specific actions, and I built the systems that drove those actions at scale.

I'm based in the Denver area and looking for senior lifecycle or growth marketing roles — remote or hybrid — where the lifecycle motion sits at the center of how the product grows.
