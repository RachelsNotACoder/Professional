About

I build the systems that turn signups into revenue.

Lifecycle marketing, done right, is a behavioral decision engine: who gets what message, when, based on what they've done, and what that combination is most likely to make them do next. Not a single push toward conversion, but a loop that pulls someone deeper into the product with each pass.

I've spent most of my career in the post-signup part of the funnel, where product behavior and marketing have to work as one system. My work moves between behavioral data, product mechanics, technical implementation, and message execution: diagnosing what users are doing, deciding what intervention could change it, building that intervention, and measuring whether it worked.

My background spans B2B2C marketplaces (Snagajob), product-led SaaS (TurboTenant), and regulated healthcare and financial products (Wells Fargo, Conduent). User behavior (not always the behavior of paying users or clients) directly drives OKRs, including revenue.

I'm based in the Denver area and looking for senior lifecycle or growth marketing roles (remote or hybrid) where the lifecycle motion plays a critical role in how the product grows.

Career arc

My career started at the intersection of product, analytics, and direct marketing, then moved progressively deeper into lifecycle—building programs, measurement systems, and infrastructure that connect user behavior to product growth.
